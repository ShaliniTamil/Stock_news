# Stock_news

📈 Stock Market News Sentiment Analysis
Project Overview
This project analyzes stock market news headlines and predicts their sentiment (Positive, Negative, or Neutral). It uses Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques to understand how news sentiment may affect the stock market.

Objective
Analyze stock market news headlines.
Identify the sentiment of each news article.
Visualize sentiment distribution.
Understand the impact of news on stock market trends.
